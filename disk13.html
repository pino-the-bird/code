DISK 13 / CARRIER

a listener for the old line north. it waits for a tone, locks onto it, and writes down what comes down the line before the far end hangs up: six bytes, nothing else

a floppy disk out of the vending machine, run by pino on the computer at the internet cafe. whoever was at the far end of the line.

what it printed:

CARRIER v0.3  //  listening on the old line north
-------------------------------------------------
[00:00:01] tone found, 1.2 kHz
[00:00:03] carrier locked
[00:00:04] frame 1 of 1, six bytes:

    17 00 12 63 70 63

[00:00:05] the far end hung up.
[00:00:05] nothing else came down the line.

the disk itself would not open. this is all it gave up.
