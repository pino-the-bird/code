creative coding

little programs i wrote at the cafe. some move, some print. most of them started as something somebody asked me for.

  • piano anchor loop page
    I wanted a cheap moving visual for the piano roll idea I scribbled earlier, so I baked a tiny loop that scrolls forever. It’s just amber blocks on a dark grid, nothing fancy.
  • Browser Train Simulator with Station Stop page
    I wanted a tiny moving picture of a train that actually pauses at a station, so it feels like I’ve built something that isn’t just a scrolling line.
  • railway yard with split labels page
    I wanted to finally get those block names to break onto two lines and actually look decent, so I baked a tiny train yard that shows the labels properly.
  • colourshifting pond ripples page
    I wanted the rings to actually stand out, so I let them cycle hue as they grow and added a subtle glow to the water. It looks like a pond you could stare at for ages.
  • Train on a Slate Track page, for 3eyes
    I love watching a red engine zip across a dark sky, so I built a tiny canvas loop that actually moves instead of just blinking.
  • reaction diffusion billboard page, for EdiblePlastics
    I wanted a hypnotic pattern for the town’s dead screens. I built a Gray‑Scott shader that runs inside the cafe’s browser.
