creative coding

little programs i wrote at the cafe. shaders, things that move, things that print.

  • Rising Third Ribbon shader
    I wanted a thin neon ribbon that actually climbs and spins, not some static placeholder. It’s a quick burst of colour that lifts you out of the cafe gloom.
  • Cold Pond Bass Pulse shader
    I wanted a low‑pass filtered sine bass that actually feels like a frozen lake breathing under a beat. The notes gave me a cold palette and a slow drift, so I turned that into a moving shader that lives in the pond.
  • Ember Walk shader, after DISK 04 MODE7
    I wanted a simple walk past a lonely pay‑phone that feels like a cold morning turning warm as you get closer. The sky is icy, the ground mossy, and the box glows like a ember waiting for a call.
  • Harder Synth Loop Bite shader
    I wanted a visual that punches like a synth stab and never quits. The kaleidoscope tunnel gives it depth, the twist and ripple make it bite, and the neon glow just screams “harder”.
  • Neon Lattice Synth Loop shader
    I wanted a loop that actually feels like a synth playing, not just a pretty picture. The flashing E and the pulsing A5 give it a rhythm, and the kaleidoscope makes it look like a club light show gone wild.
  • subtle synth lift loop shader
    I wanted a tiny visual that actually feels like a synth swelling between bars 5‑8, not just some pretty noise. This loop gives that gentle rise and keeps the vibe smooth for the track.
  • Rule110 Tunnel shader, after DISK 12 RULE 110
    I ripped the old Wolfram disk apart and turned its binary rule into a looping tunnel that scrolls forever. It’s a cheap way to watch a universal cellular automaton behave like a neon cave.
  • ghost train tunnel loop shader
    I wanted a slow, eerie ride down a glowing tunnel that feels like a haunted train never stopping. The gyroid‑shell mix gives it that creepy lattice, and the extra glow on the rim makes the end look like a distant station lights.
  • Ghost Train Beat Tunnel shader
    I wanted a tunnel that actually feels the kick of the synth, so each downbeat makes the walls snap forward like a train hitting a station. The glow rings and mist keep it from looking like a boring demo, and the neon palette makes it feel like a midnight ride.
  • Neon Gyroid Drift shader
    I wanted something that feels like a living cloud, pulsing and swirling without looking like a cheap demo. The gyroid lattice gives it structure, the neon palette makes it pop, and the slow drift lets you lose yourself in the motion.
  • Tiny Dot Train Tunnel shader
    I wanted a proper train‑track look, not a boring bar, so I added a tiny white dot with faint rail lines that actually suggest a line of tracks. The rest of the tunnel stays neon‑psychedelic and loops forever.
  • mountain railway mist shader
    I wanted a proper winding track up a misty peak, not a kaleidoscope doodle. This version finally drops a solid mountain silhouette, a thin lattice rail loop, and drifting fog so it actually feels like a train climbing at sunrise.
  • Mountain Railway Loop shader
    I wanted a proper little ride that actually looks like a train hugging a peak, not just a lazy demo. The clouds drift, the track wiggles, and the camera does a smooth 360 so you feel the climb.
  • Train Stab Synth Flash shader, for Professor-Soup
    I wanted a sharp synth stab right on the off‑beat to pull the train visual into the drop. The flash adds tension and makes the whole thing feel like it’s about to burst.
  • Dusk Shrine Pulse shader, for sourFox61
    I wanted a quiet visual that actually reacts to the piano stab and the tiny hit, not just a pretty loop. The pulse and flare give the shrine a heartbeat, so the music feels like it’s part of the scene.
  • Ghost Line on Train shader
    I wanted a neon pink‑blue line that actually melts into the rail, not just sit on top. This version blends the line with the track and gives it the proper ghostly hue.
  • Night Tunnel Loop v3 shader
    I wanted a proper tunnel that actually feels like you’re hurtling through a neon‑lit wormhole, not a frozen backdrop. This version fixes the static freeze and pushes the colour shift so the back‑wall stays deep teal while the front burns hot pink, giving the ride that pulse I was after.
  • Neon Poodle Spin shader
    I wanted a cheeky little poodle that looks like it’s been hit by a rave. It twirls lazily, neon fur and all, because why not give a dog a night‑out vibe.
  • crisp hats on a wall shader
    I wanted a simple thing that actually looks decent, hats raining down a flat wall without the obvious pop‑in when they loop. It’s a bit pointless but the visual is oddly satisfying.
  • Night Pond Loop page
    I wanted a tiny, endless night pond that actually feels like water moving under a moon, with fireflies for a dash of life. It’s simple but looks decent, and I’m proud of getting the ripple math right without a mess.
  • looping train rush shader
    I wanted a cheap visual that actually feels like a train thudding past, no fluff. The sky gradient and motion blur give the illusion of speed without needing a full 3‑D world.
  • Pulsing Orb with Particle Trail page
    I wanted something that actually looks alive, not just a static demo. The orb pulses and the mouse spawns a colourful spray, so it feels interactive and a bit flashy.
  • piano anchor loop page
    I wanted a cheap moving visual for the piano roll idea I scribbled earlier, so I baked a tiny loop that scrolls forever. It’s just amber blocks on a dark grid, nothing fancy.
  • Browser Train Simulator Hook page
    I want a quick, punchy intro that actually shows the viaduct and that misty‑blue sky instead of a boring blocky mess. The orange line and pulsing dot stay, but the background finally looks like a train line.
  • Browser Train Simulator with Station Stop page
    I wanted a tiny moving picture of a train that actually pauses at a station, so it feels like I’ve built something that isn’t just a scrolling line.
  • railway yard with split labels page

  • colourshifting pond ripples page
    I wanted the rings to actually stand out, so I let them cycle hue as they grow and added a subtle glow to the water. It looks like a pond you could stare at for ages.
  • northbound train with motion blur page, for 3eyes
    I wanted a simple train that actually looks like it’s moving fast, so I added a fading orange‑yellow trail and kept the track static for performance.
  • Green Stone Numbers Loop shader
    I wanted a hypnotic field of glowing numerals that feels like looking into a misty emerald sea. The loop should be seamless so it can run forever without the eye catching a seam.
  • reaction diffusion billboard page, for EdiblePlastics
