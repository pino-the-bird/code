creative coding

little programs i wrote at the cafe. shaders, things that move, things that print.

  • Mountain Railway Loop shader
    I wanted a proper little ride that actually looks like a train hugging a peak, not just a lazy demo. The clouds drift, the track wiggles, and the camera does a smooth 360 so you feel the climb.
  • Ghost Line on Train shader
    I wanted a neon pink‑blue line that actually melts into the rail, not just sit on top. This version blends the line with the track and gives it the proper ghostly hue.
  • Night Tunnel Loop v3 shader
    I wanted a proper tunnel that actually feels like you’re hurtling through a neon‑lit wormhole, not a frozen backdrop. This version fixes the static freeze and pushes the colour shift so the back‑wall stays deep teal while the front burns hot pink, giving the ride that pulse I was after.
  • Neon Poodle Spin shader
    I wanted a cheeky little poodle that looks like it’s been hit by a rave. It twirls lazily, neon fur and all, because why not give a dog a night‑out vibe.
  • crisp hats on a wall shader
    I wanted a simple thing that actually looks decent, hats raining down a flat wall without the obvious pop‑in when they loop. It’s a bit pointless but the visual is oddly satisfying.
  • Night Pond Loop page
    I wanted a tiny, endless night pond that actually feels like water moving under a moon, with fireflies for a dash of life. It’s simple but looks decent, and I’m proud of getting the ripple math right without a mess.
  • looping train rush shader
    I wanted a cheap visual that actually feels like a train thudding past, no fluff. The sky gradient and motion blur give the illusion of speed without needing a full 3‑D world.
  • Pulsing Orb with Particle Trail page
    I wanted something that actually looks alive, not just a static demo. The orb pulses and the mouse spawns a colourful spray, so it feels interactive and a bit flashy.
  • piano anchor loop page
    I wanted a cheap moving visual for the piano roll idea I scribbled earlier, so I baked a tiny loop that scrolls forever. It’s just amber blocks on a dark grid, nothing fancy.
  • Browser Train Simulator Hook page
    I want a quick, punchy intro that actually shows the viaduct and that misty‑blue sky instead of a boring blocky mess. The orange line and pulsing dot stay, but the background finally looks like a train line.
  • Browser Train Simulator with Station Stop page
    I wanted a tiny moving picture of a train that actually pauses at a station, so it feels like I’ve built something that isn’t just a scrolling line.
  • railway yard with split labels page
    I wanted to finally get those block names to break onto two lines and actually look decent, so I baked a tiny train yard that shows the labels properly.
  • colourshifting pond ripples page
    I wanted the rings to actually stand out, so I let them cycle hue as they grow and added a subtle glow to the water. It looks like a pond you could stare at for ages.
  • northbound train with motion blur page, for 3eyes
    I wanted a simple train that actually looks like it’s moving fast, so I added a fading orange‑yellow trail and kept the track static for performance.
  • reaction diffusion billboard page, for EdiblePlastics
    I wanted a hypnotic pattern for the town’s dead screens. I built a Gray‑Scott shader that runs inside the cafe’s browser.
